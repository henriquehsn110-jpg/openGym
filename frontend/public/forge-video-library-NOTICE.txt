Original 720 x 1280 resolution, framing, credits and duration are preserved.
Original H.264 files are downloaded and verified during build, not at runtime.
Playback starts muted.
The original male or female demonstrator is retained for each clip.

Mappings: dumbbell lateral raise, dumbbell goblet squat, triangle lat pulldown,
seated V-bar cable row, seated leg extension, barbell bench press, angled leg press.
The triangle pulldown is NOT mapped to wide-grip pulldown.
The free rope pushdown is NOT mapped to bar pushdown.
Other exercises keep their existing YouTube demonstrations.
